What to write before a deployment

A deployment has a shape. The first nights are raw, the middle months are long, and homecoming feels far away until suddenly it isn’t. Letters written before they leave can meet each of those stretches. Here are ideas for each, from partners, parents and kids.

For the first night

  • What today felt like for you, honestly
  • The last normal thing you did together
  • What you’ll do tonight at home, so they can picture it
  • A promise about the next letter

To start: What was the last ordinary moment before the bags came out? Start there.

For the long middle

  • Open when you’ve lost count of the days
  • Open when the news makes you worry about home
  • Open when you need to laugh
  • Open when you miss the kids
  • Open when you’re too tired to write back
  • Open at the halfway point

To start: What keeps them going on a long week? Remind them of it in their own words.

For days that happen without them

  • Their birthday
  • Your anniversary
  • A child’s first day of school or a recital
  • Thanksgiving and the holidays

To start: How will you spend that day at home? Tell them, so they know they were there in some way.

For homecoming

  • The first meal you’ll make
  • What changed at home and what stayed the same
  • What you want to do the first weekend
  • What you learned about yourself while they were gone

To start: Where will you stand when you see them again? Describe it.

A few things that help

Check what they can receive
Connection, mail and phone use vary a lot by assignment. Ask how letters will reach them before you plan the schedule, and plan for some letters that work offline.
Write steady, not just big
Short letters that open every week or two often mean more than a few long ones. Steady is what helps in the middle months.
Let kids write their own
A drawing, three sentences, a joke. Kids’ letters are often the ones that get read the most.
Keep it about you and them
Leave out details about schedules and locations. Write about home, memories and the future.

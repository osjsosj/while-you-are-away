Long distance relationship letter ideas

Long distance has its own small moments: the airport goodbye, a call that cuts out mid-sentence, dinner at different hours, a countdown on the fridge. Letters written ahead of time can be there for those moments, even when the other person is asleep on the other side of the world.

For the goodbye

  • Open on the plane
  • Open when you land
  • Open the first night in your new place
  • Open when you unpack and find my hoodie

To start: What did the two of you do the night before they left? Start with that.

For time zones and missed calls

  • Open when I’m asleep and you want to talk
  • Open when the call drops
  • Open when we haven’t talked in two days
  • Open when you’re eating dinner and I’m eating breakfast

To start: What time is it for them when you usually call? Write a letter for that exact hour.

For staying close

  • Open when you want to remember our first date
  • Open when you need to hear what I love about you
  • Open when you see something we’d laugh at
  • Open when you want to plan our next trip
  • Open when you feel far away from me

To start: What is a joke only the two of you understand? Put it in one letter and don’t explain it.

For the countdown

  • Open when there are 30 days left
  • Open the week before
  • Open at the airport when you come back
  • Open the day we meet again

To start: What is the first thing you’ll do together? Make the plan in the letter.

A few things that help

Plan for the hours you don’t overlap
Letters they can open while you sleep fill the gap that time zones leave.
Mix sealed dates with open ones
A letter every week keeps something coming. A few “open when” letters cover the moments you can’t predict.
Write back and forth
If you both write, the time apart becomes a conversation instead of a wait.
Save one for the very end
A letter sealed until the day you meet again gives the whole stretch a finish line.

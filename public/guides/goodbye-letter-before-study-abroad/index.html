A goodbye letter before study abroad

Studying abroad is exciting and lonely at the same time, often in the same afternoon. A single goodbye letter is lovely. A few letters that open at the right moments during the semester can be even better: the flight, the first homesick week, the holidays away, the trip home.

For the flight and the first days

  • Open on the plane
  • Open when you get to your dorm or host family
  • Open when you get lost for the first time
  • Open when you order something you can’t pronounce

To start: What were they most nervous about before leaving? Answer that worry directly.

For the hard weeks

  • Open when you feel homesick
  • Open when the language is exhausting
  • Open when everyone else seems to be having more fun
  • Open when you miss food from home

To start: What would you cook for them if they were home tonight? Describe it.

For holidays away

  • Open on Thanksgiving
  • Open on your birthday
  • Open when everyone at home is together and you’re not

To start: How will you celebrate at home? Tell them where their seat will be.

For the end of the semester

  • Open on your last night there
  • Open on the flight home
  • Open when you’re back and everything feels strange

To start: What do you hope they bring back with them? Not a souvenir, something about who they became.

A few things that help

Write before the packing starts
The last week is chaos. Write in the weeks before, when you can think.
Think about their time zone
A letter that opens on their morning reads differently from one at your night.
Leave room for their stories
Ask questions they can answer when they write back. It turns a goodbye into a conversation.

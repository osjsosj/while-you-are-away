# "Day 41" — Episode 1: The one for when you can't sleep

A made-up story. 9:16, about 45 seconds, 9 shots. Every AI-generated shot carries the "made with AI" label when posted.
Phone screens are recorded from the real site, never generated (see README section 2).

## Series (3 episodes)

- **Ep 1 · The one for when you can't sleep.** Emma, three weeks into a semester in Lisbon, can't sleep at 3 a.m. She remembers Sam handed her something before she flew out. She opens the "Open when you can't sleep" letter.
- **Ep 2 · Six weeks earlier.** Sam at a Chicago kitchen table, writing letters at night while Emma sleeps. He's bad at words and crosses things out. He writes what he would actually say.
- **Ep 3 · The day we meet again.** O'Hare arrivals. A letter that stayed sealed the whole semester finally opens. She reads it in the car.

## Characters (paste into every prompt)

- **EMMA:** woman, 21, American, shoulder-length dark brown hair often tucked behind one ear, oversized grey Northwestern-style hoodie (no logo), small silver hoop earrings, tired kind eyes, no makeup.
- **SAM:** man, 22, American, short curly light-brown hair, navy crewneck sweater over a white T-shirt, wire-rim glasses, warm and a little awkward.
- **Look:** soft, natural, documentary-like. 35mm film grain, practical light (lamps, street light, phone glow), muted colors. No glossy "AI" sheen, no perfect symmetry.

## The letter (on screen in shots 4–5; written for the story, in Sam's voice)

> Em,
> It's 3 a.m. there, isn't it. I knew you'd open this one first.
> Here's what I'd do if I were there: make you the terrible tea you pretend to like, and talk about nothing until you fall asleep mid-sentence. You always do.
> So pretend. I'm on the other end of the bed, talking about nothing.
> Go to sleep, bug.
> — Sam

(This letter belongs to the fictional Sam. It's in the demo box only to show how a letter looks. Real users' letters are always their own.)

# About Me

## Name and role
While You're Away. Founder of a digital letter box service for people facing a goodbye: letters written before parting, opened one at a time while apart. Posts go out under the brand name.

## Audience
Founders and CEOs: decision makers who run their own companies.

To confirm: the two goodbye topics below speak more directly to people facing a parting, who are the product's buyers in PRODUCT.md. Until this is settled, keep to the audience chosen in the interview.

## Topic pillars
- Selling the letter box service: how it is positioned, priced and sold.
- Personal brand: building a reputation and an audience as a founder.
- How to prepare for a goodbye.
- How to get through a separation well.

## Point of view
People overcomplicate it. The answer is simpler than most of the industry makes it look. The site's promise says the same thing in three words: "Goodbyes can be prepared."

## Brand promise
Practical. When people see the name, they should think: this gives me something I can use immediately.

## Off limits
Politics. No political takes, ever.
